Last updated: September 26, 2026

ChemOasis uses administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect accounts, usage controls, and service operation. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Report a concern

Report suspected account compromise, exposed credentials, or a security weakness through the contact information published on the ChemOasis website. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, private keys, or unnecessary personal information.

Responsible testing

Do not access another user's data, disrupt availability, use automated destructive testing, evade access controls, or publish sensitive information. Provide enough detail for ChemOasis to reproduce and evaluate the issue safely.
