About ChemOasis

Founded September 1, 2026

Mission: Make science easier to understand, accessible to learn, and exciting to explore.

Vision: Build a lasting learning environment where students, regardless of background or location, can find excellent teaching, practice, mentorship, and scientific curiosity.

Long-Term Aspiration: Establish a permanent educational presence in the United States that combines digital learning with physical spaces for science education, experimentation, mentorship, and community engagement.

ChemOasis was founded by Manoj K. Manna, Ph.D., a chemistry educator and researcher with experience in chemistry teaching and interdisciplinary scientific research.

ChemOasis combines chemistry education with intelligent learning technology to help students understand chemistry and help instructors create high-quality educational materials.
